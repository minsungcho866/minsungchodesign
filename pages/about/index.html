About

Studying at Parsons School of Design, Minsung Cho has been deeply committed to his design philosophy, which is about creating simple yet easy-to-understand design. From website design and booklet design to campaign design, he has demonstrated an exceptional ability to translate complex concepts into engaging and accessible content. Minsung’s work reflects not only his creative vision but an unwavering commitment to effective communication through design. His programs include simplicity, clarity, and his ability to deliver messages with accuracy and motivation. Whether it’s creating user-friendly websites, informative booklets, or engaging campaigns, Minsung’s work always prioritizes clarity and accessibility, ensuring that the intended message aligns with the audience. As he progresses through his academic and professional journey, Minsung remains focused on his goal of creating designs that are not only aesthetically pleasing but also convey ideas and messages to the audience.
