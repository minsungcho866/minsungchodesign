About

Minsung Cho is a graphic designer currently studying at Parsons School of Design, with a focus on clarity, simplicity, and communication. His work translates complex ideas into accessible visual systems across digital and print. Through independent and collaborative projects, he develops structured and thoughtful solutions that prioritize understanding and engagement. He has served in the Republic of Korea Army as a driver and interpreter during joint operations with U.S. forces, strengthening his ability to communicate across contexts with precision. His practice continues to evolve through experimentation and disciplined thinking.
