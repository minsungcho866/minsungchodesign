Typeface

Curvo 2

Curvo 2 is a typeface designed around the geometry of a squircle, a form that sits between a square and a circle. This hybrid shape gives the typeface its distinctive balance of structure and softness. The name Curvo is derived from the curved edges that define each character, where sharp corners are replaced with smooth, continuous curves.

Movable Type

Movable Type is a physical extension of the typeface, translating digital letterforms into resin 3D-printed movable type. Designed through Blender and fabricated using Lychee Slicer, the project consists of 40 characters, including alphabets, numbers, and punctuation.

Playing Cards

Curvo 2 Playing Cards is a playing card design project based on the custom typeface. By extending the typeface into a graphic system, the project reimagines traditional card symbols and layouts through the distinct visual language of Curvo 2.
