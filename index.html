Kinetic Time Orbiter

2026

Visualizing time is different from simply seeing it. To perceive time in a single moment is to possess a kind of control over it. Rather than separating the hour and minute hands, this object proposes a clock in which the hour hand, the minute hand, and the clock itself exist as one.

12 Rules for Life

2024

This project reimagines 12 Rules for Life by Jordan Peterson across two forms: a poster series that translates its conceptual themes into visual form and event identity, and an editorial exploration through typography and publication design. Rather than illustrating the book directly, it translates its ideas into a visual reading experience, examining how structure, hierarchy, and pacing can communicate discipline, order, and reflection.

Coal + Ice

2024

Coal+Ice is an interactive web project inspired by an Asia Society event, translating climate change into a responsive digital experience. Powered by a realtime weather API, the interface gradually melts as temperatures rise, creating a visual reflection on fragility and environmental change.

Solar System

2023

Solar System is an infographic poster design that visualizes information about the solar system and its planets. Using a network of lines to represent planetary distance and movement, the project transforms scientific information into a clear and engaging visual system.

Receipt? Rethink!

2023

Receipt? Rethink! is a campaign project encouraging audiences to reconsider the everyday use of receipt paper. Through infographic-driven poster designs, the project visualizes the environmental and material costs of production, including BPA, tree usage, and water consumption.
